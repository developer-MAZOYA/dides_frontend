About DIDES

About Us

The Dodoma Institute of Development and Entrepreneurship Studies (DIDES) is registered by NACTVET with accreditation status. The institute is located at Nzuguni Ward, about 2 kilometres from the Dodoma Bus Terminal.

DIDES offers technical training at Certificate and Ordinary Diploma level in Community Development and Agriculture Production. It also provides short courses and seminars for government officials and other public and private workers. The institute plans to offer degrees in the future.

🔭

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in public administration studies and other public service related disciplines in the country.

🎯

Our Mission

To improve the efficiency and effectiveness of public administration in Tanzania through comprehensive, competency-based training and applied research.
