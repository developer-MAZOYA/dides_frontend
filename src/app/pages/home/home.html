Welcome to DIDES

Building skills for public service and community development

The Dodoma Institute of Development and Entrepreneurship Studies is registered by NACTVET with accreditation status. We are located at Nzuguni Ward, about 2 kilometres from the Dodoma Bus Terminal.

About the institute

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in public administration studies and other public service related disciplines in the country.

Our Mission

To engage in excellent teaching and improve the efficiency and effectiveness of public administration through competency-based training and applied research.
